Your Journey

From Guest To Steward

Four stages — start by visiting, then grow into the Heralds family.

01

Guest

Visit us and connect with our welcome team — come as you are, no commitment required.

02

Membership

Express interest in joining and take your first step into the Heralds family.

03

Steward
